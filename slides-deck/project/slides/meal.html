The everyday table

Basic structure of a Korean meal

밥

Rice

bap, the centre of the meal

국 · 찌개

Soup

soup or stew, served hot

반찬

Side dishes

banchan: kimchi, eggs and more

수저

Stainless cutlery

a metal spoon and chopsticks

A typical Korean meal has four parts: rice (밥) at the centre, a soup or stew (국 or 찌개), several small side dishes called banchan (반찬) such as kimchi and egg dishes, and a stainless steel spoon and chopsticks (수저).
