Korean dining etiquette

Mind your manners

1

Before eating

Wait for the elders to start eating, then say:

잘 먹겠습니다

“Thank you for the meal”

2

During the meal

Keep your rice and soup bowls on the table. Do not lift them.

3

Drinking

Pour drinks for elders using both hands. Turn your head slightly away when drinking in front of elders.

4

After the meal

Show your thanks by saying:

잘 먹었습니다

“Thank you, I enjoyed the meal”

Four moments of Korean table manners. Before eating, wait for the elders to start and say 잘 먹겠습니다 (jal meokgetseumnida), “thank you for the meal”. During the meal, keep your rice and soup bowls on the table. When drinking, pour for elders with both hands and turn your head slightly away when you drink. After the meal, say 잘 먹었습니다 (jal meogeotseumnida), “thank you, I enjoyed the meal”.
