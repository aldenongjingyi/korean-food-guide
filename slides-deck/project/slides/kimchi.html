김치

Fermented classic

Kimchi (김치)

  • One of Korea’s most famous traditional foods.
  • Made through fermentation, mainly using vegetables such as cabbage and radish.

Common types

Baechu kimchi (배추김치): napa cabbage kimchi

Kkakdugi (깍두기): cubed radish kimchi

Other fermented foods

Doenjang: fermented soybean paste

Gochujang: fermented red chilli paste

Kimchi is one of Korea’s most famous traditional foods. It’s made by fermenting vegetables, mainly cabbage and radish. Common types are baechu kimchi, made with napa cabbage, and kkakdugi, cubed radish kimchi. Korea has other fermented foods too, like doenjang (soybean paste) and gochujang (red chilli paste).
