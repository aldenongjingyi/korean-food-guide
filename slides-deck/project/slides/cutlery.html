Spoon and chopsticks

Why stainless cutlery?

Royal tradition

Korean royalty once used silver utensils, believing they could detect poison.

Hygiene

Metal is easy to clean and does not easily absorb food odours.

Durability

Stainless steel chopsticks are strong, reusable and long-lasting.

Fun fact

Korean chopsticks are typically flat and made of metal, unlike Japanese wooden chopsticks and Chinese wooden or bamboo chopsticks.

Why do Koreans use stainless steel cutlery? Three reasons: royal tradition, since royalty used silver believing it could detect poison; hygiene, because metal is easy to clean and doesn’t absorb smells; and durability. Fun fact: Korean chopsticks are flat and metal, while Japanese ones are wooden and Chinese ones are wooden or bamboo.
